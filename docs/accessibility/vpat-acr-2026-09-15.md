## Terms

- **Supports:** the functionality of the product has at least one method that meets the criterion without known defects or meets with equivalent facilitation.
- **Partially Supports:** some functionality of the product does not meet the criterion.
- **Does Not Support:** the majority of product functionality does not meet the criterion.
- **Not Applicable:** the criterion is not relevant to the product.
- **Not Evaluated:** the product has not been evaluated against the criterion. Used only for Level AAA.

## Summary of defects to resolve

Ordered by impact; details, file locations and suggested fixes are in `wcag-review-2026-09-15.md`. Items 1 and 2 of the original list were resolved on 2026-09-15 (D-035, D-037).

3. Visible focus on favourited and conflicted blocks; a global focus style (2.4.7).
4. Text enlargement in the native builds and portrait lock (1.4.4, 1.3.4).
5. Per-screen document titles (2.4.2).
6. Status announcements for the update banner, alerts and reminder test (4.1.3).
7. Non-text contrast of the active tab icon, outlines, block boundaries and switch (1.4.11).
8. Headings for in-screen sections and ARIA clean-up on generic elements (1.3.1, 4.1.2).

Once items 1 to 3 are resolved, the expected conformance for the web build is Supports or Partially Supports on every Level A and AA criterion, with the remaining partials being 1.3.1, 2.4.2, 4.1.3 and, for native builds only, 1.3.4 and 1.4.4.
