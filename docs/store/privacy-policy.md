# Privacy Policy — Telluride Blues & Brews app

*Effective date: «effective date — not yet set».*

This policy covers the Telluride Blues & Brews Festival companion app and its web version, published by **SBG Productions** («SBG Productions legal/mailing address — placeholder»), the producer of the Telluride Blues & Brews Festival. In this policy, "the app," "we," and "us" refer to this application; "SBG Productions" is the controller responsible for it.

## No accounts

The app does not require or offer sign-in. There are no user accounts, usernames, or passwords anywhere in the app.

## What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following only on your own device, in your browser's or app's local storage — never on a server we control:

- Your favorited artists and sets
- Your personal festival plan (built automatically from your favorites) and any conflict choices you make
- Your app settings: theme (light/dark/auto), reminder lead time, whether you've turned on set reminders, and which alerts you've read

None of this leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data deletes it permanently; we have no copy to restore or delete on our end.

## What the app sends or receives over the network

- **Festival lineup and alerts.** The app reads the current festival schedule and organizer alerts from a public Firebase (Firestore) database maintained by SBG Productions. This is a read-only, unauthenticated connection — we receive nothing identifying you beyond the ordinary network information (like your device's IP address) any internet connection carries, and we do not log, store, or link that to you.
- **The web version.** The web app is served from GitHub Pages, which — like any web host — receives standard web-server request information while delivering the page. We add no analytics or tracking on top of that.
- **Notifications.** If you turn on "Remind me before my sets," the app schedules reminders directly on your device using your operating system's local notification feature. These are calculated and fired entirely on your device, not sent to us or through any third-party push service in this version.

## What we do not do

- We do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind.
- We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information, because we do not collect it in the first place.
- We do not track your location.
- We do not use cookies for tracking (the web version may use ordinary browser storage for the on-device data described above, not for tracking).

## Third parties

The app's lineup and alert data is delivered via Google's Firebase (Firestore) platform. Firebase's own privacy practices govern its infrastructure; see Google's privacy policy for how Firebase handles standard network-level information. We do not configure Firebase to collect analytics or advertising data for this app.

## Children's privacy

This app is a general festival guide, not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because the app collects no personal information from any user.
