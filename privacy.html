PRIVACY POLICY · SEPTEMBER 8, 2026

Your weekend stays yours.

The Blues & Brews festival companion does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or transmit personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your favorite artists, generated festival plan, reminder choices, cached festival content, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. Removing the app or clearing its site data removes this information.

Network use

When online, the app requests the public festival content file and static app files so it can show current announcements and save them for offline use. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party sign-in.

Notifications

Set reminders are optional and requested only when you tap “Remind me.” They are scheduled locally on your device. Denying notification permission does not limit the rest of the app. The app does not send promotional notifications.

Sharing and calendar

Share graphics are generated on your device from your selected artists. The app opens your device share sheet only after you choose to share. Calendar files are created on demand; you choose whether and where to save them.

Third-party links

The guide can open official festival and map links. Those destinations have their own privacy practices.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact SBG Productions through the official festival website at tellurideblues.com.
